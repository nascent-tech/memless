# Code of conduct

memless adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. It applies to everyone who takes part in the
project — in issues, pull requests, reviews, and any other space where the
project is discussed — and when someone represents the project elsewhere.

## In short

We want memless to be a welcoming place to contribute, whatever your
experience, background or identity. So we:

- are kind and patient, and assume good intent;
- criticise ideas and code, never people;
- accept that a pull request may be declined, and explain why when we decline
  one;
- do not harass, insult, or publish anyone's private information.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behaviour that breaks this code, write to
**open-source@nascent-tech.co**. Reports are handled in confidence; you do not need to have been the target to report.

The maintainers act on reports as the Contributor Covenant describes, from a
warning to a temporary or permanent ban from the project, depending on what
happened.
